Take USDC in person

Arc Till turns a phone or tablet into a card reader for USDC on Arc. Type an amount and show the QR code. Each sale gets its own deposit address, so the till sees the payment land and marks it final in about a second. Customers pay from any wallet, an exchange, or another chain through CCTP.

Sales go to your payout address. The till never asks for a private key.
